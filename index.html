Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Austin gate owners

What gate services does LoneStar Gate Solutions offer?

We provide complete gate solutions including custom gate installation, automatic gate repair, gate sales and equipment, access control systems, mobile welding and custom fabrication, and preventative maintenance.

Do you install and repair automatic driveway gates?

Yes. We install and repair swing, slide, cantilever, and bi-parting driveway gates using wrought iron, ornamental steel, aluminum, and cedar. We service all major gate operator brands including LiftMaster, Viking, DoorKing, Nice, and FAAC.

Which areas around Austin do you serve?

We serve Austin and the greater Central Texas area, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Westlake Hills, Dripping Springs, Spicewood, Buda, Kyle, Hutto, Manor, Lago Vista, Wimberley, Driftwood, Del Valle, and Jollyville.

Do you offer same-day or emergency gate repair?

Yes. Same-day service is available for many repairs, and we provide 24/7 emergency service. Call (737) 265‑4627 to check availability for your location.

Are you licensed, insured, and do you warranty your work?

LoneStar Gate Solutions is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. Our work includes a 1-year labor warranty plus the full manufacturer warranty on gate operators and equipment.

Can you weld and fabricate custom gate parts on site?

Yes. Our mobile welding service handles on-site gate frame repairs, custom brackets, hinge fabrication, and structural reinforcement in steel, iron, and aluminum using MIG and TIG welding.
