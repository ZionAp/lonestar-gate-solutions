Local gate service built for Lakeway

Many Lakeway driveways are sloped, curved, or shared, so a gate has to be planned carefully. We check clearance, drainage, and operator placement, then choose hardware that can handle the grade without straining. For gated communities, we also service shared entrances, telephone entry, and access control.

Lake Travis humidity and sun can wear finishes, batteries, and moving parts. We inspect hinges, rollers, and control boxes, and we recommend maintenance that keeps the gate operating smoothly. When a frame is bent or a hinge fails, mobile welding can often solve the problem on site.

Gate services we provide in Lakeway

From a single driveway gate to a full commercial entrance, we handle the gate, operator, power, and access control as one system.

  • Gate installation: custom swing, slide, cantilever, and bi-parting gates built for your entrance.
  • Gate repair: hinges, frames, rollers, tracks, operators, sensors, and control boards.
  • Automation and access control: keypads, remotes, telephone entry, video intercom, loops, and safety devices.
  • Welding and fabrication: on-site repairs, custom brackets, hinge fabrication, and frame reinforcement.
  • Maintenance: seasonal inspections, lubrication, battery checks, and adjustment for reliable operation.

Common gate problems in Lakeway

A Lakeway gate that will not open may have a dead battery, a tripped breaker, a failed sensor, or a control board issue. We test power and safety circuits first, then repair or replace only what is needed.

  • Gate alignment, hinges, rollers, and track
  • Operator power, battery backup, and control settings
  • Safety sensors, loops, and reversing behavior
  • Frame welds, posts, and mounting hardware

What to expect on a Lakeway gate service call

Every visit starts with a conversation about how the gate is used, what changed, and whether the problem is intermittent. We inspect the gate, operator, power, wiring, and safety devices, then test the full cycle before recommending work. You get a clear explanation of the repair, the parts involved, and the expected cost before we begin. After the repair, we check hinges, alignment, limits, and reversing behavior, and we walk through the operation with you. If a replacement is the better value, we say so and outline the options rather than pushing unnecessary work.
