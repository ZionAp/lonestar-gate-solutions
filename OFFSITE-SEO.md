## 1. Google Business Profile (highest impact)

1. Claim or create the profile at <https://business.google.com/> using the business phone `(737) 265-4627`.
2. **Primary category:** Fence contractor, Gate installer, Welding service, Home security system service. Add the other relevant categories as secondary.
3. **Business name:** use the real trading name only — "LoneStar Gate Solutions". Do not stuff keywords.
4. **Website:** https://lonestargate.com
5. **Service area:** Austin, TX plus the cities listed on https://lonestargate.com/service-areas.html.
6. **Hours:** enter real hours. If you offer 24/7 emergency service, state it in the description and posts.
7. **Description (750 char max):**
   > LoneStar Gate Solutions provides automatic driveway gate installation, repair, automation and welding across Austin, TX and Central Texas. Same-day service, honest pricing, and licensed & insured technicians. Call (737) 265-4627 for a free quote.
8. **Photos:** upload at least 15 real images: completed gate installations, gate openers, welding fabrication, before-after repair shots. Add a new photo every week.
9. **Services:** add each individual service with a short description and a link to the matching page on the website.
10. **Posts:** publish one Google post per week (offer, tip, recent job, or update) with a photo and a "Call" or "Learn more" button.
11. **Q&A:** seed 5–8 common questions yourself, then answer them accurately.
12. **Messaging:** enable it if someone can respond during business hours.

Tier 2 — directories relevant to Austin, TX:

For every citation, use the exact NAP above and link to https://lonestargate.com.

## 4. Review generation (never buy reviews)

Goal: a steady stream of genuine, detailed reviews on Google first, then Yelp/Facebook.

- Ask every satisfied customer the same day, by SMS and email.
- Make it one tap: link directly to the Google review form from the Business Profile.
- Respond to **every** review within 24–48 hours — thank positives, fix negatives publicly.
- Aim for 5–10 new reviews per month rather than a one-time burst.

### SMS template
> Hi [First name] — thanks for choosing LoneStar Gate Solutions. If you have a minute, could you leave us a quick Google review? It really helps local customers find us: [review link]. Thanks!

### Email template
> Subject: How did we do?
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thank you for choosing LoneStar Gate Solutions. We'd love to hear how the job went. If you're happy, a short Google review helps other homeowners in Austin, TX find us: [review link]
>
> — The LoneStar Gate Solutions team · (737) 265-4627

> **Important:** Do not offer incentives for reviews — that violates Google policy.
> AggregateRating schema stays disabled on the site until real reviews exist.

## 5. Local link building (real relationships)

- Sponsor a local youth sports team, neighborhood event, or charity fundraiser and ask for a site link.
- Join the Austin, TX Chamber of Commerce and relevant trade associations — most list members online.
- Partner with complementary local businesses (realtors, property managers, HVAC suppliers) for reciprocal links.
- Offer a genuinely useful guide to local blogs and ask to be quoted or linked.
- Submit a guest article about automatic driveway gate installation, repair, automation and welding to an Austin-area publication.

and searches that found you. Track website conversions in Google Analytics 4 with a
goal for form submits and `tel:` clicks. Review monthly and double down on what works.
